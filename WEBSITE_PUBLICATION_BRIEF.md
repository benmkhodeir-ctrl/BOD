# BEN ON DELIVERY — Website & Publication Brief

**Status:** live publication, updated 14/09/2026

## Product purpose

BEN ON DELIVERY is an operator publication and permanent home for a growing body of work about **the point where the system meets reality**.

The publication comes first. Commercial success should emerge because useful thinking earns trust and makes contacting Ben feel like the natural next step.

## Commercial philosophy

**Earn the enquiry. Don’t chase it.**

The site should provide soft, contextual pathways to contact Ben without interrupting the editorial experience or presenting BEN ON DELIVERY as an established consultancy before the evidence supports that positioning.

Long-term, the body of work should make it plausible for developers, operators, strata organisations, media and other relevant people to seek Ben’s view on parcel delivery and scalable delivery systems.

## Contact

One simple form only:

- Name — required
- Email — required
- Phone — optional
- Message — required

No public phone number. No organisation, building, job-title or enquiry-type fields. Qualification happens naturally after contact.

The form is processed through Web3Forms and may capture the source page and referrer invisibly for attribution.

## Publishing model

**Posts are field notes. Articles are the synthesis.**

Real work and useful conversations can become observations; the strongest observations can become durable articles after evidence, competing explanations and implications have been worked through.

Primary content types:

- Articles
- Field Notes

Editorial series/tags:

- The Last Inch
- The Process Broke Here
- General observation

`Find the Lever` remains an editorial lens, not a forced content series.

## Comments

Comments are part of the publication and currently use Cusdis.

- public name required
- email kept private
- no anonymous comments
- moderation before publication
- spam control
- useful disagreement explicitly welcomed

## Visual system

Use the locked Ben on Delivery identity:

- B4 Structured Break
- Anton Regular for wordmark, display and major headings
- Inter for body and interface text
- Charcoal `#1A1A1A`
- Warm off-white `#F4F1EA`
- Muted ochre `#C9922A` as a restrained signal accent
- observed operational photography rather than staged consultant imagery

The B4 break identifies a real mechanism or handoff where useful. It is not decoration.

## Evidence

Distinguish:

- documented fact
- field observation
- inference
- information gap

Current Amazon Key technical, commercial and product claims must come from current Amazon Key operational evidence, not the website or brand source.

## Current technology

- Astro static publication
- Markdown content collection
- GitHub repository `benmkhodeir-ctrl/BOD`
- production branch `main`
- Cloudflare Pages hosting and delivery
- Web3Forms for contact processing
- Cusdis for comments
- no CMS

GitHub is the canonical source. An approved commit to `main` triggers the Cloudflare production build. Netlify and Hyvor Talk are not part of the current system.

The content remains portable. Add a CMS later only if Markdown becomes a genuine publishing bottleneck.
