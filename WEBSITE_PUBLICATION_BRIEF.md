# BEN ON DELIVERY — Website & Publication Brief v3

**Status:** publication and product brief, updated 14/09/2026

## Product purpose

BEN ON DELIVERY is an operator publication and permanent home for a growing body of work about **the point where the system meets reality**.

The publication comes first. Commercial success should emerge because useful thinking earns trust and makes contacting Ben feel like the natural next step.

## Commercial philosophy

**Earn the enquiry. Don’t chase it.**

The site should provide soft, contextual pathways to contact Ben without interrupting the editorial experience or presenting BEN ON DELIVERY as an established consultancy before the evidence supports that positioning.

Long-term, the body of work should make it plausible for developers, operators, strata organisations, media and other relevant people to seek Ben’s view on parcel delivery and scalable delivery systems.

## Contact

One simple form only:

- Name — required
- Email — required
- Phone — optional
- Message — required

No public phone number. No organisation, building, job-title or enquiry-type fields. Qualification happens naturally after contact.

The site may capture source page/referrer invisibly for attribution.

## Publishing model

**Field observations are raw material. Field Notes capture shorter observations permanently. Articles provide deeper synthesis.**

Real work and useful conversations can become field observations. A field observation is not automatically a publication. It may become a social post, a Field Note, evidence inside a longer article, a question worth investigating, or nothing public.

Primary website content types:

- Articles
- Field Notes

A **Field Note** is the named short-form website publication type. It can preserve a useful observation, mechanism, question or early learning without pretending every issue has been fully resolved.

A full-length article should provide deeper synthesis after evidence, competing explanations and implications have been worked through. Several field observations, Field Notes, social discussions or questions may accumulate into one durable article.

Current BenonDelivery website publishing targets and the standing editorial run sheet are governed by `04 — BenonDelivery — Brand, Editorial & Content Playbook` in Brand & Content HQ.

Every substantive new website publication should also be distributed socially in accordance with `02 — BenonDelivery × Ripples — Social, Learning & Content Operating System`. Social distribution should adapt the insight to the platform rather than mechanically copy the website text.

Editorial series/tags:

- The Last Inch
- The Process Broke Here
- General observation

`Find the Lever` remains an editorial lens, not a forced content series.
